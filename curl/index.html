Curl · a companion on your phone

The longer you talk, the less you explain.

She is a small pearl drop with a glowing curl, living in a little house on your phone. Talk or type. Tell her about your week once, and the next talk starts where the last one stopped.

Tell me when she is outWhat she keeps
With Apple for review

iPhone first. With Apple for review, November 2026. In the store she is listed as ANITA for now. Android is being built.

Tell her once.She keeps it.

What she keeps is yours

All she knows about you is on one page.

Read it, search it, erase any line, or take a copy. Delete your account and it all goes. Never sold, never used for ads.

Read
Every line she has kept, in plain words.
Search
Look up anything she has kept.
Erase
Any single line, or say “forget that” out loud.
Take a copy
What she knows, her map, her diary and her dreams, as plain text files.

Who writes her replies

She asks before your first talk.

  • Either

    You say yes

    An outside AI model writes her replies: what you say, and what she needs to answer it, goes to the company that runs it.

  • Or

    You keep it Private

    Only our own model answers.

A video call uses the outside model, in Private too, and asks you first.

She also

A day with her.

  • 01Sees what you show her on a video call.
  • 02Writes you four cards a day and an intention to keep.
  • 03Keeps a diary with her own sketches, behind Face ID if you like.
  • 04Wears what you pick, with credits you earn by using the app.
  • 05Switches your lights, fan, AC or heater in Apple Home when you ask.
  • 06Speaks English and Tamil.
  • 07Answers to the name you give her.

Apple Health

She notices how you slept.

Switch on Apple Health and she notices your sleep, steps, resting heart rate and workouts. They show on your day page and in her morning note, and in Private mode she can talk about them with you.

Your health data is never sent to any AI company, never used for ads and never sold.

The plan

She starts on your phone. She won’t stay there.

We’re building the brain for tomorrow’s robots, and she is learning you first. When a robot body is ready, the idea is that she moves in with everything she knows.
