Underneath

One idea under all four: a brain that keeps, works and moves.

  1. 01

    It keeps

    A memory of people and of work, written as lines you can read. Curl runs on it. Through Nenai, so can the app you are building. A line can be read, and a line can be erased.

  2. 02

    It works

    In a company, agents read what came in, find what was done before and draft what comes next. They never sign. A person does, and every step is logged.

  3. 03

    It moves

    The last step is a body. We come from robotics, so we are building toward it from both ends: robot cells for the factory floor now, a robot at home later.

How we work

Four rules we do not bend.

  • 01

    Agents draft. People sign.

    Every quote, report and approval is drafted by an agent and signed by a person. AI never signs, and every step is logged.

  • 02

    What she keeps is yours.

    Everything Curl knows about you is on one page. Read it, search it, erase any line, or take a copy.

  • 03

    On your premises.

    In a company it runs on your own machines, so drawings, prices and people’s names stay in the building.

  • 04

    One honest line.

    Under each piece of work, one line says what works today and what is still the plan. No demo is passed off as a product.
