How it works

You give. It works out. A person decides.

The same three beats on every job we take.

  1. You give1 / 3

    The job someone re-types all day.

    An enquiry, a request for a quote, a folder of past projects. We start with one workflow, not the whole company.

  2. It works out2 / 3

    Agents read, match and draft.

    They read what came in, find the closest work you have done before, and write the first draft from it.

  3. The result3 / 3

    A draft, waiting for a person.

    Your team changes it, approves it or sends it back. Nothing is signed by AI, and every step is logged.

Questions

Asked often, answered plainly.

Does our data leave the building?

+

No. It runs on your own machines, so drawings, prices and people’s names stay in the building.

Does AI approve or sign anything?

+

No. Every quote, report and approval is drafted by an agent and signed by a person. AI never signs, and every step is logged.

How do we start?

+

With one workflow. We agree the success numbers on day one and measure them on day 30. If it does not clear the bar, you keep the report.

How good is it today?

+

It depends on the step, and we say so line by line. Finding the closest past project is right about half the time today, which is why a person still checks every draft.

What kind of company is it for?

+

Factories, plants and engineering teams: companies that run on repetitive knowledge work, and whose data cannot go to the cloud.
